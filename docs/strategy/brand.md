# Brand strategy

<DocReview id="BRAND" />

## Positioning to decide
The product is one plain white T-shirt. The brand must explain who it serves, what wearing it means, and why someone would choose it.

## Archetypes under consideration
| Direction | Meaning | Working message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator | The wearer supplies the expression | You bring the story. |
| Explorer | Freedom to follow your own path | Go your own way. |
| Everyperson | Confidence without status performance | Nothing to prove. |

These are proposals, not approved slogans. “White Shirt” is a project label, not an approved brand name.

## Decisions required
Choose the audience, primary archetype, brand name, promise, tone, price positioning, and product differentiation. Record decisions in the decision log and reference the approving issue comment.

## Voice proposal
Use direct, concrete language. Support product claims with evidence. Let the chosen archetype guide imagery, wording, and interaction choices consistently.

## Review questions
Which audience and archetype should lead? What practical product quality supports the emotional promise?
